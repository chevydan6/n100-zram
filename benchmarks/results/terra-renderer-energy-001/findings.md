# Renderer-page userspace package-energy pilot

Three fresh disposable headless Chromium services with temporary profiles and generated local content supplied present anonymous renderer pages. This is the same biased sampling method and generated workload as the earlier [zstd timing pilot](../terra-renderer-zstd-001/findings.md), not a representative reclaim trace. Trials 007/008/009 retained 127/123/125 non-same-word pages and recorded 6,665/6,662/6,659 failed or nonresident attempts across mapping classes. No page bytes or addresses were saved; renderer-to-document association was not independently verified.

On each page, the four userspace codecs—4 KiB-table scalar LZ4, AVX2 GHY parent, AVX2 GHY 16-word sampling candidate, and libzstd 1.5.7 level 3—ran compression and decompression in randomized order. Each operation had three warmups and a 1,024-call batch. The GHY variants shared the parent's decoder; frames and final timed output were verified. The physical N100 was on battery; the CPU0 governor and 700–800 MHz policy limits matched before and after each timing phase. There was no continuous frequency or background-load telemetry, no CPU affinity, no cycle counter, and the selected pages were repeatedly warm. Wall and thread CPU times include Python/ctypes and loop overhead.

A read-only inherited RAPL descriptor measured CPU **package** energy across each batch, including clock/counter bookkeeping and background package activity. The three completed trials had no zero batch readings. This does not establish measurement accuracy at small differences, total laptop energy or battery life. Per-page raw timing/energy values and the exact shuffled task order were not retained; only aggregate medians, median per-page ratios, zero counts and size totals are available, so this pilot cannot independently audit order or compute uncertainty intervals from raw page observations.

Across trials 007/008/009, median per-page paired compression package-energy changes versus userspace zstd were -69.6%/-68.1%/-69.0% for the GHY parent, -71.3%/-69.8%/-69.9% for the sampling candidate, and -69.5%/-69.0%/-69.3% for scalar LZ4. These are differences in measured package energy per repeatedly encoded page, not equal changes in power or battery life. For context, trial 008's absolute median package-energy readings were 50.4 µJ/page for LZ4, 52.0 for GHY parent, 47.7 for sampling GHY and 156.6 for zstd; medians of paired ratios differ from ratios of separate medians. Decode and CPU/wall metrics and all session values are in the trial JSON.

The sampling candidate's median paired compression package-energy changes relative to the GHY parent were -4.18%/-1.94%/-1.03%; the corresponding wall-time changes were -1.18%/-1.09%/-1.13%. Background activity, lack of continuous telemetry and the discarded raw page observations make these small differences inconclusive. Relative to LZ4, parent compression energy changes were +1.06%/+0.74%/-0.35%; again, no reliable advantage follows. GHY's total encoded bytes were 199,184/211,123/213,515 versus LZ4 167,637/180,302/179,703 and userspace zstd 105,755/111,576/109,034. Framing differs and totals are not allocator-rounded.

The first launcher failed its required dedicated-cgroup check because authentication moved the process; no pages were sampled. A corrected isolated user scope then exposed a division by zero because 128-call batches yielded zero energy deltas. Both failed records and the original sampler are retained. The completed 1,024-call version reports zero counts and declines an energy ratio if any relevant reading is zero. No experimental host module or stock ZRAM setting changed.

These data are userspace level-3 zstd comparisons, not stock kernel zstd, LZO-RLE or actual ZRAM measurements. They do not demonstrate a real-world acceptance gain, stable battery-life improvement, or safe native recovery. Preserve the known sampling-gate adversarial regressions; no candidate is promoted.
