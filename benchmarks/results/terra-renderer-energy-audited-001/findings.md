# Auditable renderer-page userspace energy comparison

Three fresh disposable headless Chromium services supplied sampled anonymous renderer pages from generated local content. This repeats the earlier [aggregate-only pilot](../terra-renderer-energy-001/findings.md) for the specific reason that its per-page measurements and task order were not retained. Samples were 123, 119 and 124 non-same-word pages, with 6,694, 6,623 and 6,644 unsuccessful/nonresident mapping attempts across classes. These are highly biased resident-page samples, not a reclaim trace, actual user browsing or independently identified document renderer. No page contents or addresses are stored.

The four userspace codecs and format/level settings match the earlier pilot: scalar 4 KiB-table LZ4, parent AVX2 GHY, the 16-word sampling GHY candidate, and reusable-context libzstd 1.5.7 level 3. Eight compression/decompression tasks were shuffled per page, with three warmups and 1,024 repeated calls per task; GHY candidates share the parent decoder. Timing uses Python/ctypes and repeatedly warm buffers, and the pages were sampled before the timed phase. The timing process was pinned to CPU0. Battery operation, CPU0 powersave governor and 700–800 MHz limits matched before and after timing; there was no continuous thermal, frequency or background-load telemetry.

Raw page-indexed time, thread CPU time, encoded bytes, package energy and task position are retained in each `trial-*.json`; the index does not contain a source address or page bytes. The independent audit recomputed medians, all recorded paired ratios, sizes, complete task permutations and zero counts. It passed 2,928 records in total, with 123/119/124 distinct task orders and no zero package readings. This proves arithmetic and coverage, not randomness quality, input representativeness, absence of background activity or package-energy accuracy. The auditor also passed a synthetic complete-record check and rejected a duplicate task position.

The parent GHY versus scalar LZ4 median paired compression package-energy changes were -4.73%, +7.22% and -9.27% across the three trials; corresponding wall-time changes were +6.13%, +8.22% and -8.38%. Direction varies, so the result is inconclusive. The sampling candidate versus parent had paired compression energy changes -2.32%, -0.84% and -2.54%, and wall-time changes -1.82%, -0.79% and +0.23%; these small differences are also inconclusive. For context, trial 002 absolute median package readings were 48.4 µJ/page for LZ4, 44.9 for parent, 48.1 for sampling and 163.8 for zstd. A median of per-page ratios is not the ratio of separate medians.

Parent GHY's median paired compression package-energy changes versus userspace zstd were -67.79%, -68.97% and -71.19%. Its encoded totals were 192,464 / 191,547 / 218,744 bytes versus zstd 102,918 / 98,592 / 104,529 and direct LZ4 163,660 / 159,916 / 169,337. Framing differs and sizes are not allocator-rounded. Per-trial, page-resampling intervals and all decompression/CPU data are in `paired.json`; these intervals are descriptive for the selected pages and are **not** confidence intervals for general workloads because pages and samples are not demonstrably independent or representative.

RAPL is CPU package energy for each batch, including background activity and timer/counter work. It is not whole-system energy, power or battery life. This is not the stock kernel zstd implementation, LZO-RLE, native ZRAM, actual swap latency or application responsiveness. The existing severe sampling-gate adversarial regressions remain; no implementation is promoted. Experimental native testing remains gated on verified recovery.
