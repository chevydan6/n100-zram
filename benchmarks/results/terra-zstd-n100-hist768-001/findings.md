# Zstd histogram threshold 768 candidate

This opt-in x86-64 candidate changes the source-size threshold for switching from simple to four-table histogram counting from 1,500 to 768 bytes. It preserves histogram results and is intended to test Gracemont's cache/dependency tradeoff, not to change the zstd format. Fresh userspace zstd 1.5.7 builds used matching GCC `-O3 -fPIC -march=x86-64-v3 -fno-tree-vectorize` flags apart from the candidate macro. Nine randomized same-round observations covered 26 corpora. Candidate and control produced identical frames on all 3,328 checked pages; candidate frames also decoded with the control and independently loaded system zstd, and truncation, undersized output and canaries were checked. This is finite userspace validation, not full-codec sanitizers or a kernel gate.

The [table](tables.md) shows mixed paired compression changes: +1.3% on bash executable, +3.1% on git, -3.1% on libc, +1.9% on sparse words and -0.4% on mixed pages. These short timings include Python/ctypes overhead and may reflect frequency or layout noise. The results do not establish a broad gain, energy benefit or reason to integrate this candidate into the kernel.
