# Evidence checkpoint and decision

The objective is a repeatable improvement of at least 10% in a meaningful real-world metric without unacceptable regressions. **That threshold has not been demonstrated.** Do not promote GZRAM or replace the current stock zstd ZRAM configuration. The current evidence does not prove that GZRAM could never help under actual memory pressure; it is not a completed native go/no-go comparison.

The bounded grouped-scanner/direct-packer GHY parent preserves independently decodable pages and shows large userspace advantages on synthetic sparse words. It loses compression time or size on other fixtures and produced larger payloads than LZ4 or userspace zstd in small biased compiler, SQLite and Chromium renderer samples. The 16-word selection candidate improved userspace time on several fixtures but incurred a +163% time, +177% package-energy and +29.2% byte regression on an adversarial sparse case. The zero-prefix guards and shifted/whole-page threshold variants also have retained counterexamples. These results do not justify expanding fixed-position or single-density heuristics as general defaults.

The recent [renderer energy study](../terra-renderer-energy-audited-001/findings.md) retained and audited 2,928 page-indexed operation records. GHY versus LZ4 package-energy and time differences changed sign across the three selected samples, while userspace zstd produced smaller frames and required more codec time and package energy. Its renderer samples, warm buffers, Python overhead and package-wide background activity limit interpretation. `collect.py` regenerates `evidence.json`, checks the input audit statuses and retains source hashes; it does not make these measurements representative or turn userspace zstd into stock-kernel zstd.

The instrumented guest demonstrated vector encode and decode through the namespaced ZRAM device and scalar fallback with AVX2 hidden. Guest correctness is not native SIMD cost, hard-hang safety or pressure performance. Current stock zstd, matched LZ4, lzo-rle and experimental GZRAM still need a representative same-kernel native comparison including allocator-rounded memory, CPU, energy, latency and responsiveness. Earlier native results on different kernels or configurations are historical controls, not matched proof for this configuration.

The user reported no kernel entry during the manual restart. Alternate-boot visibility and bootability remain unverified, as do independent hard-hang recovery and the existing runner's watchdog/panic/pstore safeguards. The [Fedora menu research](../terra-grub-menu-research-001/findings.md) provides a non-mutating possible manual check but does not resolve recovery. No experimental host module should be loaded and no stock boot or swap setting changed until the established recovery and restoration gates are met.

The next decision-changing step is to establish and verify recovery with the user physically present, then measure the existing best candidate progressively: exact-kernel isolated correctness, controlled kernel-inclusive measurement, and representative bounded ZRAM pressure against the active stock configuration and matched controls. If recovery remains unavailable, further work should be limited to genuinely independent correctness or methodology questions rather than repeated synthetic trials that cannot meet the acceptance criterion. No claim of production readiness or overall GZRAM success is made.
