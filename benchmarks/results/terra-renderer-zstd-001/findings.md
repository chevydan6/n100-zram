# Userspace zstd and GZRAM on disposable renderer pages

Three fresh headless Chromium services with temporary profiles and generated local content supplied the anonymous renderer-page reservoirs. The sample is subject to the [renderer study's](../terra-owned-renderer-sample-001/findings.md) severe nonresident/readability bias, unverified association of a specific renderer with the document, lack of reclaim weighting and potential repeated content. Trial 001/002/003 retained 126/120/117 non-same-word pages and recorded 6,652/6,694/6,654 failed or nonresident attempts across classes. Pages are transient in process memory and are not written. Existing stock swap and ZRAM are unchanged.

On every same page, operations for four userspace codecs were shuffled: scalar LZ4 with the 4 KiB table, AVX2 GHY parent, AVX2 GHY 16-word sampling candidate, and libzstd 1.5.7 level 3 using reusable compression and decompression contexts. Each operation received three warmups and a 32-call timed batch. The GHY candidates share the parent's AVX2 decoder. Initial round trips, final decoded bytes and final encoded-byte stability were checked. Wall and thread-CPU times include Python/ctypes and loop overhead, use repeatedly warm pages, and are not application or cold-page timings. The input/source/binary hashes and all aggregate metrics are retained in the trial JSON.

Total encoded bytes for trial 001/002/003 were zstd 101,864 / 104,541 / 111,577; LZ4 165,305 / 163,969 / 185,871; GHY parent 197,449 / 192,019 / 222,278; and sampling GHY 197,449 / 189,342 / 215,834. Thus LZ4 frames were 56.8–66.6% larger than userspace zstd across these totals, while parent GHY was 83.7–99.2% larger. Formats include their respective frame overheads; sizes are not capped or allocator-rounded.

The median per-page paired wall-time ratios relative to userspace zstd across the three trials were: LZ4 compression -69.1% to -69.2%, GHY parent compression -67.2% to -69.1%, and sampling candidate compression -68.2% to -69.9%. For decompression, LZ4 was -60.9% to -61.9% and GHY parent -62.1% to -63.5%. These are reduced times, not equal percentage increases in throughput. The raw JSON retains absolute medians, paired CPU times, and candidate-versus-parent results. Separate pooled absolute medians may not yield the median paired ratio because page costs vary.

This is libzstd level 3 in userspace, **not** a direct benchmark of the stock kernel zstd backend, which has its own page-size parameters, memory allocation and ZRAM overhead. Kernel FPU costs, package energy, cycles, frequency telemetry, battery life, application responsiveness and true swap performance were not measured. The comparison does not establish a real-world winner or the 10% acceptance criterion. The known sampling-policy adversarial losses remain and no candidate is promoted. Native experimental kernel work remains gated on verified recovery.
