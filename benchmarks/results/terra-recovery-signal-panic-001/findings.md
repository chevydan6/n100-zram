# Guest recovery signal and panic checks

Two fresh diskless, networkless KVM guests ran the exact host kernel `7.2.11-200.fc44.x86_64`. They extended the [temporary runtime-policy rollback test](../terra-recovery-transaction-guest-001/findings.md) with a deliberate termination signal during partial setup. Each guest verified that the three tested panic-related sysctls returned to their pre-test values after normal exit, injected failure, and the self-sent termination signal. Both launchers passed and their recorded host ZRAM and sysctl values matched before and after. The guest-only helper continued to refuse execution on the host. Full console logs, result records and hashes are retained.

The second guest additionally set its own `kernel.panic` timeout to two seconds and deliberately invoked the guest's SysRq crash command. Its serial log recorded the expected panic and two-second reboot countdown, and QEMU exited successfully with `-no-reboot`, consistent with receiving a reset request. The guest did not complete a subsequent boot; no host kernel panic or reset was triggered. This demonstrates the configured panic timeout on this KVM guest only, not the laptop's physical reboot route, crash-log persistence or behavior during a hard lockup.

The rollback mechanism runs in userspace and cannot restore values after SIGKILL, process destruction, a kernel panic, or a machine hang. Reboot resets runtime values but the physical reboot and restoration path is not established by this guest result. The manual power-button check under normal operation is separately recorded in [the restart check](../terra-manual-reset-check-001/summary.md). Independent hardware watchdog, EFI pstore and native panic behavior remain unverified. The native experimental module and normal boot configuration were not changed.
